# Tabloom: creator outreach

Research date: 2026-11-05. Creator list: `creator-database.csv` in this folder (26 creators; priorities A/B/C, with evidence links).

## Ground rules
- **Do not send any outreach until the app is notarized and a download page exists.** Reviewers install what you send. Right now the apps are ad-hoc signed (see the brief).
- Say what works and what doesn't in every message. Today that is:
  - **Verified on one Mac (macOS 26.1, Chrome 156, Apple Silicon):** each profile becomes its own app with its own icon, Dock tile and Cmd-Tab entry. Sessions persist. Uninstall leaves Chrome untouched.
  - **Not yet:** using your *existing* Chrome profiles with logins kept. Each app starts a fresh profile, so you sign in again; Chrome Sync brings back bookmarks and extensions.
  - **Not tested:** Intel Macs, Spaces/fullscreen/multi-monitor, Chrome-update repair.
- No "best", "only" or "first" claims. Unbundle and Parall exist; name them if asked.
- Use only contacts the creator lists publicly (the `public_contact` column). If it says "see profile/about page", use the contact route on their YouTube About page, website, or DMs if those are open. Never guess an email.
- One creator, one message, written in your own words. Personalise the first line with a specific video or post of theirs (from `similar_product_evidence_url`).
- For TidBITS, follow Adam Engst's published pitch format (subject `PITCH: Tabloom – <function>`, plain email, follow up after about a week in the same thread): https://talk.tidbits.com/t/how-to-pitch-apps-to-tidbits-in-the-ai-era/33861

## Disclosure (FTC / ASA): put this in every agreement and remind the creator
- **US (FTC):** a free licence, a discount or affiliate commission is a "material connection" and must be disclosed clearly, for example "Tabloom gave me a free licence" or "I earn a commission from this link". Vague tags such as "#collab" or "#sp" are not enough. Source: FTC, "Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers" (2019), summarised at https://www.manatt.com/insights/newsletters/advertising-law/ftc-issues-disclosures-guide-for-social-media (accessed 2026-11-05).
- **UK (ASA/CAP):** paid or affiliate content is advertising and must be obviously labelled as an ad. Read the current guide (4th edition, dated 23 Oct 2026 on the page) before running affiliate deals: https://www.asa.org.uk/resource/influencers-guide.html. The detailed label rules are in the linked PDF, which I did not read; check it.
- Never ask for a positive review, never script opinions, and never ask a creator to hide the relationship.
- r/macapps bans paid affiliate or referral links in posts, so creators should not drop affiliate links there.

**Subject:** Review copy: Tabloom, Chrome profiles as separate Mac apps

Hi {first name},

I liked your {video/post title}, especially {specific point}. I'm {your name}, and I build a small Mac utility called Tabloom.

It solves one problem: if you use several Chrome profiles, they all sit under one Chrome icon. Tabloom turns each profile into its own Mac app, with its own icon, Dock tile and entry in the normal Cmd-Tab switcher. There's no custom switcher and no Accessibility permission. It costs $4.99, one-time.

To be upfront about where it is:
- Each profile app currently starts a fresh profile. You sign in once and Chrome Sync restores bookmarks and extensions. Importing your existing profiles isn't supported yet.
- I've tested on Apple Silicon with macOS 26.1. Intel and multi-monitor setups aren't tested yet.

Would you like a free licence to try it? There's no obligation to cover it, and if you do, say whatever you actually think. If you mention it, please note that you got a free licence.

Thanks,

## Follow-up cadence
| Step | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| Initial | Day 0 | Template email or DM. One channel only. |
| Follow-up 1 | Day 7 | Reply in the same thread: 2 lines plus one *new* fact (an update, a fixed limitation). TidBITS asks for about one week. |
| Follow-up 2 | Day 21 | Last note: "Closing the loop; the licence stays valid." |
| Stop | After follow-up 2 | Mark "no response" and do not contact again for at least 3 months, and only if there's major news. |

- Never mention that other outlets covered it (Adam Engst's guidance).
- Reply to questions within 24 hours. Expect fact checks.
